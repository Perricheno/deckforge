# Speech: National Bank of Kazakhstan (about 4 minutes)

## Slide 1
The National Bank of Kazakhstan is the institution behind the tenge, and its work rests on three tasks: keeping prices stable, keeping the financial system stable and making sure that payments move safely across the country, which is why Astana sits at the center of the map and every region is connected to it.

## Slide 2
Kazakhstan is one country with one currency and one payment space, so banks and people from Atyrau to Oskemen use the same tenge and the same settlement infrastructure, while the National Bank issues the currency, oversees payment systems so that transfers settle in every region, and works to keep prices and the financial system stable.

## Slide 3
Four moments explain how the National Bank works today: independence in 1991 started the country's own financial system, the tenge was introduced on 15 November 1993 and is marked every year, in August 2015 the exchange rate began to float freely and inflation targeting became the framework, and from 2021 the National Bank has been piloting a digital form of the national currency.

## Slide 4
A rate decision reaches prices step by step, because the base rate first sets the price of money for banks, then rates on loans and deposits follow, households and firms adjust spending and borrowing, and finally inflation moves toward the target over time, while the gauge on the slide is only an illustration and not the current rate.

## Slide 5
Money now exists in several forms, since cash is issued by the National Bank, cards and QR codes serve everyday payments in shops and online, and the digital tenge is a digital form of the national currency that is still in pilot, and all of them run on shared rules and oversight so that trust in the tenge does not depend on the payment method.

## Slide 6
Rate and inflation move together but with a lag, because when prices rise faster than the target the rate goes up first and inflation reacts later, after loans, spending and expectations adjust, so policy works over months rather than days and clear communication helps to shape expectations, and the curves shown here are illustrative, not published data.

## Slide 7
In one line, stability is the product: price stability is the main objective behind every rate decision, trusted payments give banks and people one reliable infrastructure, and the digital tenge and instant payments build on the same foundation. Thank you. We are ready for questions.
