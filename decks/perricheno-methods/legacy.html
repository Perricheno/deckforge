Main research question

To what extent does an agent-based document generation service improve the efficiency, quality and perceived usefulness of academic writing for students, and which factors explain continued use?

RQ1 · OUTCOMES

How long does it take to get a compiled document, and how often does it compile without repair?

RQ2 · PERCEPTION

How do students rate usefulness, ease of use, trust and satisfaction?

RQ3 · ADOPTION

Which user and document factors relate to repeated use and intention to continue?

DesignQuantitative survey
SampleAbout 100 students
PeriodAbout 3 months
DataSurvey + usage logs

Why not other approaches
Qualitative interviews: rich but slow, small N; our aim is breadth.
Mixed methods: needs more time than the project allows.
Randomized experiment: no realistic group that uses no tools.
Limits: non-random sample and self-report; we report associations, not cause.

Safeguards for AI-assisted analysis

  • Privacy: only anonymized data goes to a model.
  • No invented numbers: every statistic is computed by code; the agent only writes the interpretation.
  • Exploratory means exploratory: discoveries are labeled as such, corrected for many comparisons and confirmed on new responses.
  • Human final say: agents suggest, authors decide; plan, code and prompts are saved.
